# The magnum opus

The Great Work: the full sequence that carries the matter from its raw state to
the [philosophers' stone](/substances/philosophers-stone.md). The operations vary
between authors; the *colour sequence* is remarkably stable.

## The four stages

| Stage | Colour | Sense |
|---|---|---|
| Nigredo | black | putrefaction, reduction to [prima materia](/substances/prima-materia.md) |
| Albedo | white | washing, purification |
| Citrinitas | yellow | dawning of the solar principle |
| Rubedo | red | the completed stone |

Later writers frequently drop citrinitas, giving a three-colour scheme. The
compression is worth noting: it tracks a shift from observing the vessel to
allegorizing the soul.

## Why colour

Colour change is the one signal a pre-instrumental laboratory can read reliably.
It is reproducible, it is public, and it does not depend on the operator's
judgement. Building the whole framework on the colour sequence was, given the
available instruments, a reasonable methodological choice.

## Operations

The twelve gates assign operations to the stages. The three documented here:

- [calcination](/processes/calcination.md) — nigredo
- [dissolution](/processes/dissolution.md) — nigredo into albedo
- [coagulation](/processes/coagulation.md) — rubedo
