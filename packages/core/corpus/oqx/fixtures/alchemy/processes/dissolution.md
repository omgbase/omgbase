# Dissolution

Taking the calx into a liquid — *solve* of the maxim *solve et coagula*. The
second gate, and the operation that produced most of alchemy's real chemical
discoveries.

element:: water
reversible:: yes

## Solvents

The search for solvents is where the practical payoff was. Distilling vitriol
and nitre gave the mineral acids, and those genuinely dissolve things nothing
else will:

```text
nitre + vitriol      → aqua fortis     (nitric acid)
aqua fortis + sal    → aqua regia      (dissolves gold)
```

*Aqua regia* — royal water, because it dissolves the royal metal — is the single
most consequential thing to come out of the laboratory tradition. It is a real
reagent, it does something no other liquid does, and its discovery was driven
entirely by the transmutation programme.

## The alkahest

The hypothetical universal solvent, said to dissolve any substance into its
prima materia. Never produced. The obvious objection — that no vessel could hold
it — was raised at the time and answered with the claim that it dissolves only
what is imperfect.

This is unfalsifiable, and its defenders were not troubled by that.
