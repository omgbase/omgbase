# Coagulation

The *coagula* half of *solve et coagula*: bringing a dissolved substance back to
a solid, ideally more ordered than before. Crystallization, precipitation, and
slow evaporation all count.

element:: earth
reversible:: yes

## Why it closes the work

Dissolution destroys structure; coagulation rebuilds it. The pair is the engine
of the whole programme — the claim being that matter repeatedly dissolved and
recoagulated is progressively refined, and that enough cycles yield the
[philosophers' stone](/substances/philosophers-stone.md).

The claim is false, but the intuition is not absurd: recrystallization *does*
purify, and each cycle *does* leave a cleaner product. The error is extrapolating
an asymptote into a transformation.

## Practical notes

Slow is better. Rapid cooling gives small, impure crystals that trap solvent;
slow evaporation at a stable temperature gives large clean ones.

```text
saturate hot → filter while hot → cool undisturbed → harvest → repeat on liquor
```

Repeating on the mother liquor is where the yield is. See the
[February notes](/lab/2026-02-notes.md) for a worked run.
