# Alchemy

The art of transformation — part proto-chemistry, part philosophy, part
contemplative practice. This repository collects notes on its substances,
processes, practitioners, and primary texts.

## The tria prima

Paracelsus reduced the material world to three principles:

- [[mercury]] — the volatile spirit
- [[sulphur]] — the combustible soul
- [[salt]] — the fixed body

## Substances

- [prima materia](/substances/prima-materia.md) — the undifferentiated first matter
- [the philosophers' stone](/substances/philosophers-stone.md) — the goal of the Great Work

## Processes

The [magnum opus](/processes/magnum-opus.md) organizes the operations into
stages. Individual operations are documented separately:

- [calcination](/processes/calcination.md)
- [dissolution](/processes/dissolution.md)
- [coagulation](/processes/coagulation.md)
