# Mercury

Quicksilver: a metal that is liquid at room temperature, and therefore the
obvious emblem of everything that refuses to hold a fixed form.

symbol:: ☿
melting_point:: -38.8
toxic:: yes

## As a principle

In the tria prima, mercury is the volatile spirit — the part of a substance that
can be driven off and recondensed. It stands opposite [[salt]], the fixed body,
with [[sulphur]] mediating.

| Principle | Role | Fixed? |
|---|---|---|
| Mercury | spirit | no |
| Sulphur | soul | partly |
| Salt | body | yes |

## As a material

Mercury dissolves gold and silver into amalgams, which made it indispensable to
assayers and irresistible to alchemists: a substance that visibly *takes gold
into itself* invites the inference that it can also give gold back.

It is also poisonous, a fact the laboratory notes record without connecting it
to the tremors and mania that follow long exposure. See the
[January notes](/lab/2026-01-notes.md) for handling precautions.
