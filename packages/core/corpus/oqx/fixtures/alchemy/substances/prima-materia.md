# Prima materia

The *prima materia* is the formless first matter from which all substances are
composed — not a reagent you can order, but the state a substance is reduced to
before it can be rebuilt.

state:: undifferentiated
obtainable:: no

## Descriptions

The texts are deliberately evasive. It is called the dragon, the chaos, the
lead of the philosophers, the vile black earth. Practitioners took this
multiplicity as proof that the *prima materia* is a condition rather than an
ingredient.

> That which is below is like that which is above.

The [Emerald Tablet](/texts/emerald-tablet.md) is usually read as describing
this reduction and return.

## Relation to the Great Work

Reduction to the *prima materia* is the purpose of [[nigredo]], the blackening
that opens the [magnum opus](/processes/magnum-opus.md). [Calcination](/processes/calcination.md)
is the usual first operation.

## Open questions

- Whether the "chaos" and the "lead of the philosophers" readings can be
  reconciled, or whether they simply belong to different schools.
- How far the term can honestly be traced back through the Alexandrian corpus
  before it becomes a later retrojection.
