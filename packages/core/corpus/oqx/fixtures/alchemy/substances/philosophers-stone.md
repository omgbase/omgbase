# The philosophers' stone

The *lapis philosophorum* is the end of the [magnum opus](/processes/magnum-opus.md):
a substance said to transmute base metals into gold and to perfect whatever it
touches.

appearance:: red
also_known_as:: lapis
transmutes:: lead

## Claims

Three powers are attributed to it, in descending order of plausibility:

1. **Transmutation** of lead or mercury into gold.
2. **Medicine** — the *panacea*, curing illness and extending life.
3. **Perfection** of the practitioner, the stone being a mirror of the soul.

The third reading dominates the later literature, where the laboratory work is
explicitly allegorical.

## Preparation

Every account is incomplete, and deliberately so. The common skeleton:

- reduce the matter to [prima materia](/substances/prima-materia.md)
- carry it through the four stages of the [[magnum-opus]]
- fix the volatile and volatilize the fixed

Compare [[mercury]], whose duality the stone is supposed to resolve.

## Open questions

- The surviving transmutation claims have never been gathered in one place with
  dates and provenance attached.
- The shift from literal to allegorical readings is well documented; what caused
  it is still disputed.
