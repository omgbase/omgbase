# Salt

What remains. Burn a plant to ash and leach the ash with water, and the residue
that crystallizes out is the salt — the incombustible, fixed body.

symbol:: 🜔
soluble:: yes
toxic:: no

## As a principle

Salt is the third of the tria prima: the body that gives a substance its
persistence and form. Where [[mercury]] flies off and [[sulphur]] burns, salt
stays behind.

The operation that produces it is straightforward and repeatable, which gave the
principle an evidential weight the other two lacked. You can *show* someone the
salt of a plant.

## Practical extraction

The standard route, still sound:

1. Incinerate the material completely — see [calcination](/processes/calcination.md).
2. Leach the ash with distilled water.
3. Filter, then evaporate slowly to crystallize.

[Coagulation](/processes/coagulation.md) is the general name for step 3.

## Note

The alchemical "salt" is not sodium chloride but the mixed carbonates and
potash left after combustion. The terminology predates the distinction.
