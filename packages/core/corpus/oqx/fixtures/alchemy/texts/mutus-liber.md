# Mutus Liber

The "silent book," La Rochelle, 1677: fifteen engraved plates and essentially no
text. The entire Great Work, allegedly, in pictures.

length:: fifteen plates
attributed_to:: altus

## Contents

The plates show a couple gathering dew in sheets stretched between poles,
processing it through vessels, and attending a furnace, framed by angels and
zodiacal signs. A sequence is clearly intended. What it instructs is not clear.

The dew-gathering is the one concretely readable operation, and it connects to a
real strand of practice: dew was held to be a celestial condensate and a candidate
[prima materia](/substances/prima-materia.md).
