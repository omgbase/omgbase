Mutate 3.6 — `set`/`unset frontmatter.<key>` edits **one entry** of the frontmatter and leaves every other byte alone (`spec/mutate` §11.3, §11.10 "Frontmatter entries"). Through 3.5 the effect re-serialized the whole mapping (`compose(parse(previous) patched)`), so a `set` of one key turned a flow list into a block sequence and dropped quoting and comments on every other key (the attn `set frontmatter.cues` finding). The planner (`mutate/effects.ts` `patchFrontmatterKey`, `mutate/frontmatter-parts.ts`) now cuts the document's head into parts by a byte-exact lexical line rule — the opening fence, one part per column-0 `key:` entry with its continuation lines and the blank/`#` lines that lead it, the trailing lead run, the closing fence — replaces exactly the key's own lines (`stringify({ key: value })` in the fence's line endings, the key's leading comment kept, duplicates removed), inserts a new key after the last entry (before trailing comments; before the closing fence when no entry exists), removes an unset key's part, and removes the frontmatter with the separator's first line ending when only the fences would remain (the pinned `unset-last-frontmatter-key` bytes). A document without frontmatter composes a fence as before. **No schema change**: a revision's frontmatter stays one blob (`spec/store` 15.0) — the per-key-parts storage encoding was measured on a reference branch and found to cost more per revision than it saved (the parts index, not the blob, dominates); storage is a later delta-encoding round. Fixtures: `spec/mutate` `effects.json` 97 → 104 (`set-fm-leaves-other-parts-byte-identical`, `set-fm-continuations-and-comments`, `set-fm-quoted-and-duplicate-keys`, `set-fm-crlf`, `set-fm-empty-fences`, `set-fm-non-mapping-body`, `unset-fm-keeps-comments-and-fences`); `spec/surface` `examples.json` (the `plan` ladder's frontmatter rungs keep `tags: [process, fire]`; three `plan_id`s moved, the `execute` rung's digest in its description and `tool-descriptions.json`) and `interop.json` (two authored `execute` digests re-pinned); `spec/cli` `docs.json` (`omg meta`'s diff keeps `tags: [text, images]`). The Rust twin follows (`omgbase-mutate` planner + a `frontmatter_parts` module; `descriptions.rs` regenerated).
