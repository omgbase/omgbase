The Rust twin of store 17.0 slice 3a (`changes/store-17-heads.md` is the TypeScript reference's note; a store major ships both engines together). `omgbase-store` reads and writes a 17.0 database byte for byte as the reference does: `schema.rs` at 17 with migration step 17 — the three `rowid`-preserving rebuilds of `docs`, `blocks` and `resurrection_pool` under `foreign_keys = OFF`, backfilled from the 16.x history tables (the live heads' frontmatter resolved once through the recorder's new `frontmatter_at_revision`, the block bytes from `blobs` by hash — a missing blob aborts — the timestamps from the `revisions × commits` / `block_changes × commits` joins), and an 18 refused; `observe.rs` mints the head (`docs.head`, D1) at the point the recorder's revision row was minted through 16.x and hands it to `HistoryRecorder::record_revision` (`RevisionTransition.head`; `record_move` is gone), writes `docs.frontmatter_raw` and `blocks.raw`/`trivia` inline (D10) and the `created_at`/`updated_at`/`deleted_at` carry rules (D6: carried on `same`, the pool's `created_at` on resurrection, the evicted foreign row's across a cross-document move; a tombstone sets `deleted_at`/`updated_at` and keeps the head); every current read — `reconstruct`, `block_raw(conn, block_id)`, `load_mut_doc`, `load_old_match_blocks`, the macros' `links_repair` scan over `instr(bl.raw, ?)`, `inbound_links_to`, the retarget planner, the materializer's `bytes_for` — comes from the rows and never from `blobs` or the delta resolver; `move_path_in` is a transition (D11: a new head, `UPDATE docs SET path, head, updated_at`, the unchanged tree captured as a revision at the new path — skipped when the same request also rewrites the document — the Receipt and the plan preview listing the moved document first, the `rename` pending row carrying the new head and `file_hash`); Every spec runner (store incl. `migrations::v16-to-v17-heads-inline-bytes-time`, mutate, surface, sync, cli, interop across the four pairings) is green against the 17.0 fixtures.

The Rust twin of slice 3c, self-contained recovery payloads (M4). `omgbase-store`: `schema.sql` a byte copy of the spec's — `pending_materializations` carries `head` (no foreign key; `rev_id` gone), `rendered_hash`, `prior_hash`, `commit_id` (no foreign key) and `created_at`; `schema.rs` `migrate_v17_pending` (the `rowid`-preserving rebuild: `head = rev_id`, `prior_hash` = the `seq − 1` revision's `rendered_hash` resolved once from the 16.x `revisions` by one self-join, `NULL` for a `remove`, `created_at` = the commit's `ts` or the migration's clock) runs after the identity half in step 17 and on open for a 3a/3b database at 17 (detected by the column set). `PendingRow { head, rendered_hash, prior_hash, commit_id, created_at }` and `supersede` (exported) pin `spec/store` §5.7 "Supersession": a `write` over a `write` replaces the target and keeps the EARLIEST `prior_hash`, over a `remove` inherits its `rendered_hash` as prior, over a `rename` keeps the rename and replaces its target; a `rename` or a `remove` replaces the row; `insert_pending` applies the rule against the table's row at the path. `effects.rs` `record_intent` builds the rows in effect order the way the reference's `Map` does — a `write` row's `prior_hash` is the `file_hash` its guard validated (`None` for a created document), a `rename`'s the moved document's `file_hash` unless a `write`/`rename` still owed at `from` lends its prior (that row deleted in the transaction), a `remove`'s `head`/`prior_hash` NULL — every row stamped with the commit's `ts`. The materializer renders from the current rows and recognises the engine's own stale file by the row's `prior_hash` (`own`); the recorder's `rendered_hash`/`previous_rendered_hash` seams are gone from `HistoryRecorder` and `history/recorder.rs`; the retention plan no longer pins a revision for a pending row (§7.1 rule 2 retired); the claims prune probes `pending_materializations.head`. `omgbase-sync` `recover_repo` compares the disk to `docs.file_hash` (no `revisions` join) and heals through the new `Store::healing_ingest`, which drops the row the replay kept in the same transaction (the file wins). Runners (store, mutate, sync): the pending projection's columns in the reference's order and the new invariant C8 (a `write`/`rename` row names a live document's current head and `rendered_hash == file_hash == sha256(reconstruct)`; a `remove` row a tombstoned document with no head and no prior; `created_at` the commit's `ts`).
