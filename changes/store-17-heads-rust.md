`move_path_in` is a transition (D11: a new head, `UPDATE docs SET path, head, updated_at`, the unchanged tree captured as a revision at the new path — skipped when the same request also rewrites the document — the Receipt and the plan preview listing the moved document first, the `rename` pending row carrying the new head and `file_hash`); The Rust twin of slice 3c, self-contained recovery payloads (M4). `omgbase-store`: `schema.sql` a byte copy of the spec's — `pending_materializations` carries `head` (no foreign key; `rev_id` gone), `rendered_hash`, `prior_hash`, `commit_id` (no foreign key) and `created_at`; `schema.rs` `migrate_v17_pending` (the `rowid`-preserving rebuild: `head = rev_id`, `prior_hash` = the `seq − 1` revision's `rendered_hash` resolved once from the 16.x `revisions` by one self-join, `NULL` for a `remove`, `created_at` = the commit's `ts` or the migration's clock) runs after the identity half in step 17 and on open for a 3a/3b database at 17 (detected by the column set). `PendingRow { head, rendered_hash, prior_hash, commit_id, created_at }` and `supersede` (exported) pin `spec/store` §5.7 "Supersession": a `write` over a `write` replaces the target and keeps the EARLIEST `prior_hash`, over a `remove` inherits its `rendered_hash` as prior, over a `rename` keeps the rename and replaces its target; a `rename` or a `remove` replaces the row; `insert_pending` applies the rule against the table's row at the path. `effects.rs` `record_intent` builds the rows in effect order the way the reference's `Map` does — a `write` row's `prior_hash` is the `file_hash` its guard validated (`None` for a created document), a `rename`'s the moved document's `file_hash` unless a `write`/`rename` still owed at `from` lends its prior (that row deleted in the transaction), a `remove`'s `head`/`prior_hash` NULL — every row stamped with the commit's `ts`.
