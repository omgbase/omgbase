Teaching surfaces (spec/mutate 3.3, spec/surface 4.4, spec/cli 4.3). The catalog's words are the interface, so three channels now teach it. (1) The `plan`, `apply` and `execute` ladders are rewritten in the 3.2 sugar — bare document paths as targets, `$new.0` bare, `-> ` leaders with template literals instead of `{ oqx }` — and every rung is still executed by the `examples.json` suite (the 4.3 digests of the unchanged rungs are pinned; `plan` fits in 900 characters). (2) The plan is the explanation: `preview.summary` is one deterministic line per resolved effect in words — `insert 2 blocks at the end of section "Risks" in /x.md`, `set frontmatter.verified = false in /substances/salt.md`, `create /lab/march.md (1 block)`, `move /a.md → /b.md (retarget 2 links in 1 document)`, `check task b_9 in /x.md`, … — specified as a wording table (§11.9), outside the digest, byte-identical across engines; the Receipt of `apply`, `execute` and every sugar tool carries the same `summary`, and the CLI prints the lines under `ok committed` (every sugar verb and the four document verbs). (3) The server teaches at the moment of failure: error `data` gains `fix`, a literal corrected call (canonical JSON) built from the request, never a rule — `conflicting_effects` (the effects minus the earlier one, or the selector narrowed to one hit), `ambiguous_target` (the first identity in the slot and the selector narrowed), `selection_too_large` and a missing `expect.max` on one-shot `apply` (the same request with the exact count), a refused selector source (spliced over the span), a `-> ` in a non-expression slot (the member spelled literally), `stale_plan` and `plan_mismatch` (re-pin the revision, or `apply` over the same effects/selector), `target_missing { symbol }` (the `create` moved first, the symbol renamed), `block_not_container` on `blocks_append` (the `blocks_insert { to, at: { after } }` call), `parent_missing`/`ambiguous_heading` on `docs_append` (the `blocks_append { id }` call with the candidate). Fixtures gain `summary` on every committing step and `fix` on the error steps that have one; both engines byte-identical, interop 118/118 across the four pairings.
