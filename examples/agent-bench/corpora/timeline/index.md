# Meridian programme

A mountain observatory built over three years, 2026 to 2028: six projects, each a
chain of milestone notes linked by `after:` (the note before it), weekly syncs under
`meetings/`, the decision log under `decisions/`, monthly journals, the glossary,
the risk register and the vendor notes. #summit

## Projects

- [Aurora spectrograph](/projects/aurora/index.md) — The first-light instrument: a bench spectrograph fed from the dome through a slit mask.
- [Basalt site works](/projects/basalt/index.md) — Road, piers, drainage: everything that has to exist before a dome can stand on the ridge.
- [Cirrus dome and enclosure](/projects/cirrus/index.md) — The enclosure: steel, shutter, thermal sealing, and the slit that has to agree with the spectrograph.
- [Delta data pipeline](/projects/delta/index.md) — From detector frames to a public archive: ingest, calibration, quick-look, API, release.
- [Ember power and cooling](/projects/ember/index.md) — Generators, substation, glycol loop and chillers for the summit.
- [Fjord partnerships and outreach](/projects/fjord/index.md) — Partners, schools, the visitor centre and the inauguration.

## Conventions

A milestone carries `when` (its date), `window` (first day..last day, inclusive),
`status` (open, done, blocked, dropped), `owner` (a people note), `track`,
`effort` (story points) and `after` — the milestone before it in the project's
sequence. Done milestones carry `closed`, the day the status flipped.
