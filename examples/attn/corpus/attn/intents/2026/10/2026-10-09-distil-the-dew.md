# Distil the May dew

Twelve flasks gathered from the linen sheets at dawn. Distil slowly in the bain-marie; the third day's run turned cloudy last year.

When any work of the Opus has the attention, the dew should come to mind: the bain-marie wants checking every hour it is lit.
