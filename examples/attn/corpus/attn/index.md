# attn

What has your attention right now? This folder is an attn workspace: a week in the life of Mireille Vautrin, an alchemist.

- `focus.md` — the **Focus** pointer: which intent is in focus (`focused_intent`), since when, and the interrupt return stack. One document, rewritten by every switch; its revisions are the only history there is.
- `intents/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD-<name>.md` — one document per **Intent** (`type: /terms/types/intent.md`, `status: active | released`), named for the day it was declared. Not necessarily tasks; some are never done. An intent's **Cues** — when it should come back to mind — are entries of its `cues:` frontmatter list (`on: focus | time | query-enter | query-exit`), not documents.
