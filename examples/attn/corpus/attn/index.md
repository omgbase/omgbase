# attn

What has your attention right now? This folder is an attn workspace: a week in the life of Mireille Vautrin, an alchemist.

- `intents/` — one document per **Intent** (`type: intent`, `status: active | released`): what she has declared into her attention. Not necessarily tasks; some are never done.
- `cues/` — one document per **Cue** (`type: cue`): something to come back to mind under relevant circumstances — a trigger (`focus`, `time` or `query`) plus the text to show.
- `current.md` — the **Focus** pointer: which intent is in focus, since when, and the interrupt return stack. One document, rewritten by every switch.
- `stream/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — the **Stream**: the append-only daily log of focus transitions from which durations fall out.
